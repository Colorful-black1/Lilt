Desktop lyric window WM_NCHITTEST and layered attributes

locked:   hit=-1 layered=True key=0 alpha=0   flags=1
unlocked: hit=1  layered=True key=0 alpha=215 flags=2

flags=1 is LWA_COLORKEY: the locked state is fully transparent and click-through.
flags=2 is LWA_ALPHA at 215/255: the unlocked state uses the semi-transparent card.
Window positioned at 300,430 with a 900x190 client area; tested point (690,450).
