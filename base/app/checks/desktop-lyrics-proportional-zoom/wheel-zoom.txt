Desktop lyrics wheel zoom check

The lyric window was resized to 900x190, then WM_MOUSEWHEEL delta=+120
was delivered to the window. One notch scales both axes by one factor.

start:               900x190
wheel up 1:         954x201
wheel up 2:         1012x212
wheel up 3:         1073x224

wheel down 1:       1010x212
wheel down 2:       952x201
wheel down 3:       897x188

The text is scaled from the window height, so the size changes above
also change the lyric font. The two axes always move by the same factor.
