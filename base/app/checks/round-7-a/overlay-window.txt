The locked flag is what makes the entire card click-through. The header and
transport row are GONE in the locked state, which is why the requested height
drops from 441 px to 234 px. Android's resize animation leaves alpha=0.8 on the
locked window in this capture; the normal unlocked window is fully opaque at the
window level.

The position is saved in lilt_settings as floating_x/floating_y. Dragging the
header updates both values, and a recreated service restores them. The card is
kept inside the display with a 24 dp top margin and a 28 dp bottom margin so the
transport row stays above the gesture/navigation bar. A display-size or
orientation change re-fits the window through FloatingLyricsOverlay.onScreenChanged.
