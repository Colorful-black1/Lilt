Android missing-file flow: database before and after re-linking

Setup: a fake MediaStore row (mediaId 999000001, "Missing Evidence") was
inserted into `songs` and referenced by playlist "Missing Test", a favourite
with 9 plays, a lyric at +250 ms and a bookmark. The app then scanned with
the real 32-song library.

After scan (before re-link)
  songs with mediaId 999000001: 0
  missing_songs:
    999000001  Missing Evidence  Music/LiltTest/99 - Missing Evidence.mp3
  playlist_songs:
    (playlist 1, 999000001, position 0)
    (playlist 1, 1000000017, position 1)
  song_state: 999000001 favorite=1 plays=9
  lyrics:     999000001 offset=250 ms
  bookmarks:  999000001 "Missing chorus" at 8000 ms

The scan moved the stale row into missing_songs and deleted it from songs,
while the playlist and all user data kept the old id.

After choosing "Glass Horizon" (mediaId 1000000017) in the re-link dialog
  missing_songs: []
  playlist_songs:
    (playlist 1, 1000000017, position 1)
  song_state: 1000000017 favorite=1 plays=9
  lyrics:     1000000017 offset=250 ms
  bookmarks:  1000000017 "Missing chorus" at 8000 ms

The playlist already contained Glass Horizon at position 1. The UPDATE OR
IGNORE + delete-old step kept that row instead of creating a duplicate, so
the playlist now contains one occurrence of the same song. Favourite, play
count, lyric offset and bookmark all moved to the new media id. The missing
screen then reported "Nothing is missing".
