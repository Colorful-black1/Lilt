The size-rule false-positive check
  The generated 3-second index tracks in Music/LiltIndex have several equal
  byte sizes. An early build grouped 30 of them into 8 false groups. The rule
  was narrowed to normalised title + artist + length only, and a unit test
  (same_size_and_length_alone_is_not_enough / sameSizeAndLengthAloneIsNotEnough)
  guards it. The final Android scan reports only the real Glass Horizon pair.
